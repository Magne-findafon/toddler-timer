About

Toddler Timer is a simple visual timer for babies and toddlers who can't read a clock yet. You choose the time; your child sees a bar of soft color that slowly shrinks. When the color is gone, the time is up. Less color, less time.

When your 2-year-old asks “Is it time to go yet?” or “When is Grandma coming?”, minutes and clocks don't mean much. A bar of color that slowly disappears does: they can see for themselves how much waiting is left.

It's made for families who keep screens to a minimum, so it behaves more like a lamp or an hourglass than an app: nothing to watch, nothing to play with, nothing to tap.

FAQ

How do I use it?

Tap 5, 10, 15 or 30 minutes, or type your own minutes and seconds and press Start. Put the phone or tablet where your child can see it. Tap the screen to pause; hold “Stop” for about 1.5 seconds to end early, so a curious toddler can't stop it by accident.

Why are there no numbers or sounds?

Small children understand “how much is left” better than numbers. The small time display in the corner is for you, and you can switch it off. A soft chime at the end is available but off by default.

Will the screen stay on?

Yes, in modern browsers (iPhone and iPad with iOS 16.4 or newer, Android Chrome, desktop Chrome and Edge). For long timers, plug the device in.

Does it keep time if I lock the phone or switch apps?

Yes. The timer follows the real clock, so when you come back the bar shows exactly how much time is left.

Can I add it to my home screen?

On iPhone or iPad: tap Share, then “Add to Home Screen”. On Android: open the browser menu and choose “Add to Home screen” or “Install app”. It then opens full-screen without browser bars.

What can I use it for?

Waiting and everyday transitions: “when the green is gone, we leave for the playground”, counting down until Grandma arrives, bedtime, taking turns with a toy, waiting for dinner, tidying up, or the end of play time.

Is it free? Do you collect any data?

It's free, with no ads and no account. There are no cookies, no analytics and no tracking. Your settings (sound, time display, last custom time) are stored only in your own browser.
